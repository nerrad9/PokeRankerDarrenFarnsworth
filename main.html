PokeRanker

What is PokeRanker?

PokeRanker is a simple tool that allows you to sort through the now 1,025 Pokemon and get a very quick ranking of your favorites.

How do I use PokeRanker?

PokeRanker will show you 25 Pokemon at once and all you have to do is select your favorites from the bunch! At any time you can click the rankings button to see your current ranking.

How does PokeRanker produce its rankings?

PokeRanker uses a multiplayer implementation of the Elo System. After you see a pokemon for the first time, it is given an Elo rating of 1500. Then based on if you selected it or not, it is given a win, loss, or draw comparing it to the other Pokemon listed.

Why 25 Pokemon, why not 1v1 matchups?

There are, as of the time of making, 1025 Pokemon(frustratingly many for fans of clean powers of 2). If PokeRanker were to use head to head matchups, it would take ~513 comparisons just to match most Pokemon once. By using this group matchup method, Pokeranker expects to rank most of the Pokemon once within ~67 trials(not a joke, the math actually just actually ended up here). This also allows PokeRanker to more accurately rank new Pokemon more accurately. PokeRanker will always give 10 already ranked Pokemon, this is for two reasons. It allows PokeRanker to further rank Pokemon already seen, while also giving a yardstick for the new Pokemon to rate against.
